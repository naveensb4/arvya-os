— Defaults Arvya will apply

Honor the "personal" label

Anything labelled personal in Gmail or marked private in Calendar is invisible to the brain. Even you can't search through Arvya for it.

Read-only by default

Arvya can compose drafts and surface answers — but never sends, schedules, or edits without your explicit approval. Each connector has its own write toggle.

Forget on disconnect (30-day grace)

Disconnect a source — Gmail, Slack, anything — and every memory derived from it expires within 30 days. Force-purge anytime in Connectors.
